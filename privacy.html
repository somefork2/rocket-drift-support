Privacy Policy

Rocket Drift Racing (“the game”). Effective date: October 13, 2026. Applies to version 17.0 and later for iPhone and iPad.

Data we collect

The game has no accounts and asks for no name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location. Your progress — coins, crystals, rockets, upgrades, records and settings — is stored only on your device and is not sent to us. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous usage analytics described below.

Purchases

Packs of crystals and coins are bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details, only the fact that a pack was bought.

Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played and what to improve. It collects gameplay events (screens opened, which track, rocket and difficulty were played, race times and results, upgrades and items bought with in-game currency), purchase events (which pack), an app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app language and an approximate location derived from your IP address by Google. The data is not linked to your identity. Data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and kept for up to 14 months.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites. Versions before 17.0 showed ads; version 17.0 removed the advertising software entirely.

Deleting data

Settings → Reset progress erases the progress stored on the device; removing the game erases everything it stored.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text and its effective date are published on this page.
