Rocket Drift Racing

Drift racing on space tracks for iPhone and iPad: 80 tracks on four planets, time attack and duels, 8 rockets, a research lab and a paint workshop.

Support

Questions, ideas or a bug? Write to somefork@gmail.com — we usually answer within two days. It helps if you mention your device and the version shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

FAQ

How do I steer? The rocket always flies forward by itself and has no brake. Hold the left half of the screen to turn left and the right half to turn right. It keeps sliding the way it was going, so start every turn early. The flight school can be replayed from Settings.

I played the old version. Is my progress gone? No. Coins, rockets and cleared tracks from versions 16.x are carried over the first time you open version 17.

A race is too hard. Before the start the game shows whether your rocket is strong enough. Upgrade it in the Garage, research nitro and shields in the Lab, take a boost from Supplies, or pick an easier difficulty.

I bought crystals or coins and they did not arrive. They are added as soon as the App Store confirms the payment. If the purchase was interrupted, close the game and open it again with the internet on: unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. If that does not help, write to us.

Can I move my progress to another device? Progress, coins and crystals are stored on the device only. Deleting the game erases them, and purchased coins and crystals cannot be restored after that.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game need the internet? No. Everything except the shop works offline. There are no ads.
